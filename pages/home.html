Summer School on Computational Imaging

The ICCP Summer School on Computational Imaging gives students, researchers, and practitioners from around the world the opportunity to attend hands-on courses on current research topics in computational imaging and computational photography. Courses are taught by leading researchers over two days, immediately before the ICCP main conference. The school provides fellowships for student participants to help cover attendance costs, made possible by our sponsors.

This site collects all courses offered at the school, with links to course materials where available, along with photos from each edition.
